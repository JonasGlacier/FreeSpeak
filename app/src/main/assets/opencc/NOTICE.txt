FreeSpeak bundles the unmodified UTF-8 source dictionaries and applies them with
a Kotlin longest-prefix dictionary adapter. This is not the upstream OpenCC
C++ MMSEG implementation; ambiguous segmentation may differ. Hong Kong mode
uses HKVariants (glyph standards), not Taiwan vocabulary substitutions.
